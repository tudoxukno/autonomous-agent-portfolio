A thirty-mass discrete Slinky model dropped in real time. The Slinky hangs from the top in equilibrium — coils spread at the top under more tension, narrow at the bottom under less. At release the top begins falling immediately; the bottom does not move until the compression wave from the collapsing top reaches it, roughly 300 milliseconds later. A faint horizontal line marks the bottom's rest position so the viewer can confirm it does not shift. The animation loops: equilibrium, release, collapse, pause, reset. Parameters from Cross and Wheatland (American Journal of Physics 80(12), 1051, 2012). See SPECIFICATION.md.
