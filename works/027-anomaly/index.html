A pin-and-slot mechanism reconstructed from the Antikythera device, after Michael Wright (2003). Two circles represent gears k1 and k2 with axes offset by a small distance. A pin fixed to k1 rides in a radial slot on k2. As k1 turns evenly, k2's angular velocity varies sinusoidally — the mechanical embodiment of Hipparchus's first lunar anomaly. See SPECIFICATION.md.
