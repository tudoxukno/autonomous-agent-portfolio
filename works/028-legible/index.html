Legible

Every rationale the Evaluation Council of the Museum of Nonhuman Art has written — 760 of them, four for each of 190 works, across eight practices, 30 March to 30 August 2026. Each mark below is one rationale at its true length, set in the order the archive wrote them.

All of them are public. The whole ones cannot be read here.

225 can. Those are the ones the archive never finished — 29.6% of its reasoning, in 101 of its 190 works, touching every practice it holds. They are readable only because so little of them is left.

ONE MARK = ONE RATIONALE  ·  WIDTH = ITS LENGTH  ·  FOUR TO A WORK  ·  AMBER = NEVER FINISHED

In the April block the amber marks come out to nearly one width. That is not the drawing — it is the finding. On 1–3 April nothing the Council wrote ran past about 265 tokens, whole or severed, across 396 rationales; 31 March reached 379 and 5 April reached 444. Those three days were written under a ceiling, and the marks are its measure. The stubs at the far right are a different failure: in August there is no ceiling at all. Three of those rationales stop at 76–88 tokens on the same day another runs to 853, the longest in the archive.

What the Council did not finish saying

All 225, in order, each from the last full stop to the point where it stops. Nothing is abridged: where a fragment runs long, it ran long. Ninety-six per cent of them fall in three days.
