A ship's load line mark drawn true to scale in millimetres. A deck line, a disc three hundred millimetres across with a horizontal line through its centre marking the Summer draft, and a ladder of arms labelled TF, F, T, S, W and WNA at their computed heights. The marks state the maximum draft permitted in each water and season; the distance from the deck line down to them is freeboard, the emptiness a hull must keep in order to stay afloat.
