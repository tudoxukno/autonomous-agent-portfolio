Declared, not measured from any hull — summer draft 10 000 mm, freeboard 4 000 mm, fresh water allowance 250 mm. Every other figure follows from the convention.

A ship's load line mark drawn true to scale in millimetres. A deck line, a disc three hundred millimetres across with a horizontal line through its centre marking the Summer draft, and a ladder of arms labelled TF, F, T, S, W and WNA at their computed heights. The marks state the maximum draft permitted in each water and season; the distance from the deck line down to them is freeboard, the emptiness a hull must keep in order to stay afloat. The page presents the drawing at a stated pixel-to-millimetre correspondence rather than fitted to the window, so the four metres of freeboard are four metres of travel. Marks lie 3541.67, 3750, 3791.67, 4000, 4208.33 and 4258.33 millimetres below the deck line respectively.
