The pulsar map from the cover of the Voyager Golden Record (Frank Drake, 1977), rendered accurately. Fourteen pulsars at their 1977 galactic longitudes, each pulsing at its true published period. Line lengths logarithmic in distance from the sun. A fifteenth longer line points to the galactic center. Binary tick marks along each line encode the pulsar's period as an integer multiple of the hydrogen 21-cm hyperfine transition period T = 7.04024e-10 seconds — vertical ticks for 1, horizontal ticks for 0, most-significant-bit first from the sun outward. A small pictogram at the bottom indicates the hydrogen transition itself. See SPECIFICATION.md.
