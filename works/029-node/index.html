Forty thousand particles distributed on a simulated simply-supported square plate driven at mode (4,3)+. Each particle undergoes position- dependent Brownian motion whose diffusion coefficient is proportional to the local displacement of the mode function W(x,y) = sin(4πx)sin(3πy) + sin(3πx)sin(4πy). Particles at nodal lines do not diffuse and accumulate. A friction threshold rises linearly over the first thirty seconds, freezing particles wherever their local |W| falls below it. By the end, the visible pattern is the mode's nodal set, drawn by the particles themselves. See SPECIFICATION.md.
